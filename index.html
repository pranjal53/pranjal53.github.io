Pranjal Bhatia

Postdoctoral Research Associate | Multi-Agent Robotics

I am a Pilot Deployment Program (PDP) Project Engineer at the Australian Institute of Marine Science (AIMS), working on systems engineering and technology integration for large-scale coral reef restoration. I hold a joint PhD in Robotics from Queensland University of Technology (QUT) and IIIT Delhi, specializing in decentralized multi-agent control, collision avoidance, connectivity maintenance, and formation coordination. My research experience spans autonomous aerial and marine systems, swarm robotics, and safety-critical control, bridging theoretical research with real-world robotic applications.
